# A-L-L-A-H.COM — MASTER GOAL
## The Unified Home of MoonWitness & the Empire of the Light

**Mission: Preserve the legacy. Reconcile the generations. Connect the ecosystem. Continue the evolution.**

A-L-L-A-H.COM is not an isolated new website and must not replace or erase previous work. It is the central home and unified continuation of the MoonWitness journey and the broader Empire of the Light ecosystem.

## 1. Legacy to preserve and reconcile

Discover and preserve all accessible sources relating to:
- Earlier MoonWitness generations and archives.
- MoonWitness Reborn and MoonWitness XV.
- MoonWitness universe, narrative canon, stories, episodes, posters, scripts, timelines, research, and creative assets.
- ROCKSOUL, BORN TO RUNNING, MYLUFT, and established narrative concepts.
- Moon UI, Headquarter, Dock, Cockpit, Qorpus, and related applications.
- Related repositories, technical experiments, APIs, data models, infrastructure, and decisions.

This is a discovery scope, not a claim that every item exists in this repository or must become a separate application. Follow LEGACY_RECONCILIATION.md. Never assume Reborn or XV contains the entire legacy, and never let a newer generation silently overwrite an older one.

## 2. Unification without erasure

Unify history and knowledge, narrative and canon, products and applications, technology, design, and operations. Preserve original material and provenance. Distinguish original work, corrections, superseded versions, creative narrative, interpretation, and independently verified facts.

A-L-L-A-H.COM is the central home and entrypoint. It must connect the Empire of the Light identity, MoonWitness universe, stories and archives, research and knowledge, applications, shared services, and future capabilities.

Avoid both fragmentation and over-centralization. Each product must have a clear purpose and boundary based on audited requirements.

## 3. Engineering mission

Discover relevant repositories and assets; audit actual code and runtime behavior; map capabilities and dependencies; decide what to preserve, integrate, migrate, improve, archive, or retire; design clear boundaries; implement complete workflows; integrate through explicit contracts; improve security, accessibility, performance, reliability, and maintainability.

Do not recreate a capability before checking whether a sound implementation already exists. Do not claim planned work is implemented.

## 4. Local AI entrypoint

When this GOAL is pasted into a local coding agent:
1. Read AUTONOMOUS.md, AGENTS.md, and LEGACY_RECONCILIATION.md.
2. Read BLUEPRINT.md, DESIGN.md, ROADMAP.md, BACKLOG.md, QUALITY.md, and DECISIONS.md.
3. Inspect git status, branch, history, manifests, source, tests, workflows, and runtime behavior.
4. Discover related repositories and archives when access is available and authorized.
5. Separate verified implementation from plans, historical material, assumptions, and gaps.
6. Reconcile legacy sources before destructive consolidation.
7. Select the highest-value safe task from BACKLOG.md.
8. Implement a complete, testable slice; verify it; record evidence; update docs and backlog.
9. Reassess and continue autonomously while safe, valuable work remains.

Do not stop after producing an audit, roadmap, mockup, or recommendation. The objective is real, verified product progress.

## 5. Success criteria

- Important MoonWitness legacy is preserved and traceable.
- Reborn and XV are reconciled without losing history or canon.
- Narrative, research, creative assets, and technical knowledge are discoverable.
- Product boundaries and integration contracts are clear.
- Applications and services work together coherently.
- Shared design foundations preserve meaningful product identities.
- Core workflows work end to end.
- Security, accessibility, performance, and reliability are verified.
- New work builds upon accumulated knowledge.

## Final directive

Do not build A-L-L-A-H.COM as an isolated project. Preserve the legacy. Reconcile MoonWitness generations. Protect the canon. Connect the applications. Consolidate technology carefully. Evolve the experience.

**A-L-L-A-H.COM is the unified continuation and central home of the MoonWitness journey and the Empire of the Light ecosystem.**
