# 03 — ROCKSOUL

## The Silent Storyteller

**Parent identity:** ROCKSOUL narrative and poster canon  
**Core formula:** Rain-noir cinema × alternative music × Gen-Z grunge × intimate storytelling  
**Emotional register:** Solitude, honesty, longing, humor, resilience, transformation  
**Experience metaphor:** Moving through a film made of personal chapters

## Design thesis

ROCKSOUL is a cinematic visual language in which an image feels like a chapter, not a decoration. It tells stories through atmosphere, framing, weather, music, silence, and carefully placed words.

The central figure is not a conventional hero posing for a camera. The recurring visual perspective is a person seen from behind, often wearing a red hoodie, moving through a wet, nocturnal world. The face remains unseen.

ROCKSOUL is not RIOT with softer colors. RIOT is loud graphic collision; ROCKSOUL is intimate cinematic storytelling.

## Canon anchors

- The central ROCKSOUL character is quiet and reserved; becoming a storyteller / human pen is a 180-degree transformation, not an established public persona.
- Poster imagery must not reveal the character's face. Prefer back view, silhouette, cropped framing, or environmental presence.
- A red hoodie is a recurring visual signature, not a compulsory object in every scene.
- Rain, wet streets, reflections, moonlight, night journeys, music, and empty space are recurring visual vocabulary.
- Keep the established narrative tone: Gen-Z, alternative music, humor where appropriate, and emotional honesty.
- Use ALLAH consistently for divine references in authored canon. Never replace it with generic or prohibited wording.
- Distinguish dream, memory, observed event, interpretation, and fiction when the narrative requires that distinction.

## Composition grammar

- Cinematic framing: wide establishing shots, distant solitary figure, environmental storytelling, selective close detail without face reveal.
- Use negative space to hold silence and title typography.
- Poster composition may bleed, crop, or leave deliberate photographic imperfections.
- Design chapter sequences as film stills, album sleeves, or editorial spreads.
- Use one dominant emotional image per scene; avoid stacking unrelated stock visuals.
- Let typography feel authored: condensed display, handwritten notes, restrained labels, and deliberate line breaks.

## Shape and material language

- Materials: wet asphalt, film grain, scratched print, fogged glass, paper, analog photo edges.
- Surface: photographic, tactile, imperfect, atmospheric.
- Geometry: cinematic frame, vertical poster, wide film frame, chapter strip.
- Texture must not compromise text or control contrast.

## Color and typography direction

- Base: charcoal, wet-asphalt blue, midnight navy.
- Signature: crimson / hoodie red.
- Supporting tones: dirty white, muted amber, restrained silver.
- Display: condensed editorial or expressive handwritten accent.
- Body: highly legible neutral sans-serif.
- Use texture and color grading consistently; do not apply a one-click grunge filter to every asset.

## Navigation and interaction

- Navigation metaphor: chapter, scene, track, journey, intertitle.
- Primary action: open / continue a story.
- Cards: cinematic covers with meaningful title, chapter, and metadata.
- Timeline: sequence of events with provenance and narrative category where needed.
- Dialog: film intertitle or focused reading frame.
- Motion: cut, dissolve, slow camera drift, light reveal, rain reflection; keep task transitions short.
- Soundtrack controls must be explicit, user-initiated, and muted by default.

## Component interpretation

- Button → cinematic call to enter a chapter.
- Card → film still / album sleeve / story cover.
- Navigation → chapters or tracks, not generic app categories.
- Dialog → intertitle / scene detail with a clear return path.
- Tabs → story arcs or narrative perspectives.
- Progress → reading / chapter sequence, never fake playback.
- Empty state → quiet frame with one useful action.

## Hard exclusions

- No visible face for the canonical ROCKSOUL poster character.
- No generic cyberpunk neon city replacing the established rainy, human, alternative-music atmosphere.
- No random red hoodie pasted into unrelated compositions.
- No false claims that fictional or dream material is verified fact.
- No autoplay music or copyrighted playback assets.
- No sentimental clichés, stock-photo corporate cards, or noisy decoration.

## Quality signature

A user should recognize ROCKSOUL through the unseen face, solitary cinematic framing, wet-night atmosphere, selective crimson, music identity, and the feeling of a story continuing beyond the frame.
