# 02 — LUNAR CHRONICLES

## The Living Universe Archive

**Parent identity:** MoonWitness Universe  
**Core formula:** Lunar cartography × astronomical instruments × living chronicle × RPG exploration  
**Emotional register:** Wonder, curiosity, discovery, memory, continuity  
**Experience metaphor:** Exploring an observatory that archives a living universe

## Design thesis

MoonWitness should feel like a universe that can be explored and remembered. It combines the precision of an observatory, the wonder of a celestial atlas, the progression of an RPG, and the depth of a narrative archive.

This world is not a generic purple space dashboard. Its identity comes from orbit, coordinate systems, lunar cycles, astronomical instruments, layered maps, and the discovery of connected stories.

## Composition grammar

- Use orbital, radial, constellation, coordinate, and cyclical arrangements when they improve orientation.
- Let timelines curve, wrap, or align to lunar cycles when the information supports that representation.
- Create a clear distinction between global universe navigation, local chapter navigation, and archive records.
- Build visual layers like an atlas: overview, region, object, record.
- Use star maps and celestial diagrams as structured information, not random decoration.
- Provide a conventional list or table alternative for dense content and assistive technology.

## Shape and material language

- Geometry: arcs, orbital rings, coordinate grids, eclipse masks, constellation links.
- Materials: dark observatory surfaces, lunar silver, etched instrument marks, translucent chart overlays.
- Depth: layered map planes and restrained atmospheric distance.
- Icons: original astronomical instruments and navigation marks; do not rely on tiny abstract symbols alone.

## Color and typography direction

- Base: midnight indigo, deep navy, charcoal.
- Accent: lunar silver, muted copper, violet, starlight.
- Display: cinematic serif or refined display face for chapter/episode identity.
- Body: neutral readable sans-serif.
- Data: monospaced coordinates, dates, observation values, and identifiers.

## Navigation and interaction

- Navigation metaphor: orbit, constellation, observatory console, archive index.
- Primary action: discover, enter, observe, unlock, or continue a narrative chapter.
- Timeline: lunar chronology with explicit dates and time zones.
- Progress: quest/state progression only where defined by the product's canon.
- Archive: source, author, date, version, provenance, and verification status must remain inspectable.
- Motion: orbital movement, star connection, eclipse transition, chart focus; avoid perpetual spinning.
- Provide static map, text navigation, and reduced-motion alternatives.

## Data integrity and canon boundaries

- Measured astronomical data must come from a named, reproducible source and retain timestamp, timezone, location assumptions, and uncertainty where applicable.
- Do not fabricate moon phase, distance, perigee, declination, eclipse, or event values.
- Clearly label fictional lore, symbolic interpretations, puzzle mechanics, and narrative synchronicities as narrative.
- Do not present numerical coincidence as scientific causation or proof.
- Preserve historical MoonWitness generations and provenance; do not silently rewrite Reborn or XV material.
- Keep canon, interpretation, research hypothesis, and verified fact distinguishable.

## Component interpretation

- Button → observatory command or chapter entry.
- Card → celestial object, chronicle record, quest, or transmission.
- Navigation → orbital map or constellation index.
- Dialog → focused observation / archive detail.
- Tabs → coordinate layers or archive categories.
- Progress → quest constellation or defined chapter sequence.
- Timeline → lunar chronology with explicit dates.
- Data visualization → astronomical plot with units, source, and timestamp.

## Signature entry experience

A navigable lunar atlas anchors the page. A selected celestial object opens a chronicle: timeline, verified observations, narrative records, and related quests. The interface reveals depth progressively rather than placing every feature on one screen.

## Hard exclusions

- No fake astronomy, unsourced precision, or invented event rarity.
- No blending of real observations with fictional narrative without explicit labels.
- No generic galaxy wallpaper as a substitute for a spatial information model.
- No tiny illegible constellation labels or color-only state indicators.
- No loss of historical source attribution.

## Quality signature

A user should recognize LUNAR CHRONICLES through orbital navigation, precise celestial cartography, layered memory, and the coexistence of wonder with evidence discipline.
