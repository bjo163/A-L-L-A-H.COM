# Artwork Direction

## Reading and Dials

- Imperium Lumen: monumental architecture for a calm traversal experience. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.
- Lunar Chronicles: navigable observatory and archive for exploration. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2.
- ROCKSOUL: intimate rainy-night cinema for personal story chapters. ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2.
- Nexus Paradox: a contextual instrument for system operation. ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2.
- Riot Anarchy: controlled zine and manga collage for expressive channel navigation. ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 3.
- Mycelium: connected organic structures for ecosystem exploration. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2.

## Material and Composition

| World | Signature composition | Material treatment | Motion purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Imperium Lumen | Axial portal, nested halls, long perspective | Layered stone planes, bevels, restrained metallic seams | Slow light reveal only |
| Lunar Chronicles | Eclipse, orbiting instruments, charted constellation | Lunar relief, etched coordinate rings, observatory glass | A single orbit clarifies the celestial mapping |
| ROCKSOUL | Distant hooded figure, wet street, framed horizon | Film grain, rain, fog, reflected crimson | Rain and reflection establish weather and distance |
| Nexus Paradox | Central instrument with contextual sensor planes | Technical glass, clipped plates, machined edge details | A scan marks the instrument focus, not a system status |
| Riot Anarchy | Layered print panels and torn graphic silhouette | Halftone, ink overlap, tape, paper wear | Brief print reveal supports the cut-and-paste rhythm |
| Mycelium | Branching network with connected clusters | Translucent membranes, mineral texture, spores | A restrained path reveal explains connectivity |

SVG is the shared source for scalable compositions and decorative detail. Public SVG and PNG previews are generated from the same server-rendered component. Public PNGs remain for metadata consumers in Gate, Dock, Headquarter, ID, Jizz, Harbor, and MoonSky. Texture is kept subtle and local so it does not obscure copy or controls.

## Composition Rules

- Keep the page task and primary action on a readable plane.
- Use one dominant visual gesture per composition.
- Preserve the ROCKSOUL character's unseen face.
- Use no sacred/religious imagery, fake astronomy, invented status signals, copied manga characters, or proprietary HUD conventions.
- The hero art is decorative; captions and all controls remain real, accessible interface content.
