# Design direction

Reading this as: an Indonesian public ISP storefront for households checking real coverage and service plans, in a cartographic network-utility style, with ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

- World metaphor: a neighborhood network atlas that clarifies routes and service handoffs.
- Palette: deep ink, warm chalk, and restrained amber; amber marks the primary action and real network nodes.
- Composition: editorial route diagrams and service-first labels, not a generic dashboard or fictional geographic map.
- Material and type: crisp ink-on-paper surfaces, measured linework, Inter for readable copy, and tabular numeric labels.
- Motion: brief feedback only; abstract route artwork remains static and ambient effects stay user-controlled.
- Asset: owner-approved original abstract topology, explicitly not a map or coverage claim. Reuse the existing E-ISP emblem.
